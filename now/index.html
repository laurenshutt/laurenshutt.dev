May 22, 2026

Work

I currently working for the Office of Innovation and Partnerships at Virginia Tech. I’ve decided to put a pause on freelance work for now so that I can dedicate more time to personal projects.

Side Projects

Code-related First and foremost, this website has been something I’ve been trying to finish for at least a couple of years. It feels like there’s always more to do, but to be honest that’s part of the fun.

  • I am in the process of adding a digital garden inspired by Maggie Appleton.
  • To improve my current workflow (which is making direct edits to the site’s source files), I’m deploying Directus as a headless CMS and connecting it to a Supabase database that will manage storage. Because Directus is self-hosted, I’m using Render to run it. This will be especially helpful when I start adding digital garden content.
  • I’m also looking at other options for hosting that can provide server-level configuration and better deployment control.
  • I’ve begun to build out a Stardew Valley page and set up a Twitch stream.

I’m just learning about the IndieWeb and have fallen down the rabbit hole there. You’ll see a lot of their conventions and on my site.

I have fallen in love with Notion and would like to spend more time building out my pages. Particularly a budget page that can show my current balances using Plaid API and Make. I have a Zettelkästen research page that I am preparing to publish as a template. Eventually I’d like to do this with some of my other pages.

Non-code-related I’ve got a few home projects going on at the moment. One is a faux fireplace I’ve been going back and forth over how to finish. At first I wanted to overgrout and paint thin brick for a old, textured look. That would however require a wet saw. So I think I’ve decided on stucco, inspired by the rock guy’s work, particularly this video.

I also really enjoy the process of rosary making, and have a couple in progress. I’ve toyed with the idea of putting some on Etsy, but that would be more for the idea page.

Hyperfixations

I’ve been reevaluating and attempting to streamline my workflows recently. I’m obsessed with other developers’ colophons or /uses pages to see what people smarter than me are using. I’ve also become fixated on naming conventions and comments styles as I try to organize my code in a consistent way.

I have an ongoing reading project on religion and gender. As an exvangelical, I’m fascinated by the original gendered contexts in which biblical texts were written and how they’ve been appropriated in different settings. Relatedly, I’ve somehow missed taking any classes on feminism and am making up for lost time. Right now I’m working my way through Susan Griffin’s writings.
