About me Download résumé

Hi there, I’m Lauren! 👋🏻 A self-taught developer driven by relentless curiosity and a love for designing imaginative corners of the internet. If you’re a CliftonStrengths person, you can read my report here. Since 2016, I’ve been building the web for Virginia Tech, currently in the Office of Innovation and Partnerships, with additional responsibilities for the main vt.edu website.

I was born in Germany to military parents and grew up riding polar bears in the wilds of Alaska. I studied anthropology for both my bachelor’s and master’s, and spent time in Germany taking courses in American Studies post-graduate-degree. After a young adulthood marked by vitamin D deprivation, I’ve finally put down roots in the temperate rainforests of Appalachia, where my family history goes back to the 1700s (one of my favorite things to talk about!).

I’ve completed award-winning work for a variety of K-12 and higher education organizations, including the Anchorage School District, Georgetown University, The Catholic University of America, and The Augustine Institute. Outside of work I am an avid hobby collector. You can find me tending a heavily-modded Stardew farm, crafting, reading, writing, or being chided by two underfed cats and a pittski. 🐾
